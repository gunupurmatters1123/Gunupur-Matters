OUR PURPOSE

A local issue should not have to become a forgotten issue.

Roads, drainage, sanitation, street lighting, water supply, public spaces and other everyday concerns can directly affect the quality of life in a community.

Gunupur Matters creates a structured place where such concerns can be documented, communicated and followed up.

OUR COMMUNITY
THE STORY

Built around a simple idea.

People who live in a place are often the first to notice when something is not right.

A broken streetlight may make an area less safe at night. A damaged road may affect hundreds of daily journeys. Waste, drainage or water concerns can quickly become community-wide problems.

The idea behind Gunupur Matters is simple: make it easier for those observations to be communicated clearly and followed through.

WHAT WE DO

From observation to follow-up.

The platform is designed around the journey of a community concern—not simply the act of submitting a form.

01

Listen

Give citizens a clear place to describe problems they see in their neighbourhood or community.

02

Document

Capture useful details so that a concern is clearer, more specific and easier to understand.

03

Follow Up

Provide a reference-based way to check the status of an issue that has already been submitted.

04

Inform

Help create greater awareness of the everyday concerns that affect local communities.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Simple values. Practical purpose.

01

Accessibility

02

03

04

A COMMUNITY PERSPECTIVE

The people who live here know what matters here.

Local knowledge is valuable. Residents see the small changes, recurring problems and everyday challenges that may not always be visible from a distance.

Gunupur Matters is intended to provide a practical channel for those observations.
